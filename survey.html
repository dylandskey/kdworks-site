AI Adoption & Opportunity Survey

Where could your work be easier?

Help us understand where your work could be easier, faster, or more effective. We’ll ask about everyday tasks first, then your experience with AI tools. No AI knowledge is needed, and it is fine if you have never used AI or do not think it would help.

Allow about 7 to 10 minutes. Please answer based on a typical recent workweek. Estimates and brief examples are enough. Do not include customer or employee names, confidential records, passwords, or other sensitive details. Names and email addresses are not requested. Your role and written examples may still make you identifiable.

The purpose is to identify workflow improvements and useful support. AI may be one option; clearer processes or better use of existing tools may be another.

Your answers are sent to the KD Works team by email through a form service and are not shown publicly. Questions marked * are required.
