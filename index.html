AI audit

Start with how AI is already used

Most teams already use AI, approved or not. Before we change anything, we find out what's happening and what you want to happen.

  1. 1

    How you use AI today

    Which tools people use, for what, and how often, including ones nobody formally approved.

  2. 2

    What you want from it

    The tasks that eat time and the ideas your team already has.

  3. 3

    Where it helps most

    We sort the opportunities by how often the task comes up, how much time it takes, and how risky it is. Some are quick wins. Some can wait.

  4. 4

    Safe, shared use

    What should never go into an AI tool, where a person needs to review the result, and how the team can work the same way.

Tell us what you need About 7 to 10 minutes.

What you get: a short written summary of what we found and a few recommended first steps. If something needs a custom build, we'll scope it with you.

Custom builds

When your tools can't do the job

Sometimes the problem is bigger than the tools you have. We build small, focused things that fit how your team already works, starting with the simplest version that solves the problem.

  • A booking page and a shared calendar, built from the spreadsheet your crew already uses.
  • An intake form that writes a short summary for your staff before each appointment.
  • A way to sort incoming messages by type, with a draft reply ready for someone to review.
  • A tool that pulls the details out of an invoice and drafts the entry for someone to approve.

These are examples, not a menu. Every build starts with your problem, and a person approves anything that touches customers, money or records.
