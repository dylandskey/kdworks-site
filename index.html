What we do

Ways we can help

Most teams don't need more software. They need the tools they already pay for to work together. Here is where we usually start.

  • Workflows and automation

    We map a process your team repeats every week, then build it in the tools you already use: intake, approvals, reports, and handoffs between Drive, Sheets, Asana and Slack.

  • Email management

    Calmer inboxes. Labels and rules that sort the mail for you, reply templates, daily or weekly priority summaries, and a cleanup routine that sticks.

  • AI training for teams

    Hands-on sessions for ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot and Gemini. What each one is good at, how to ask well, how to use them safely, and prompts your whole team can share.

  • AI opportunity assessment

    A short survey and a conversation to find where AI would actually help. We rank ideas by how often the task repeats, how much time it takes, the risk, and the value.

  • AI policy and guardrails

    Clear, simple guidance for your team on what is encouraged, what needs a second look, and what is off limits, so people can use AI with confidence.

  • Tool integration

    We connect the systems you already use so the same information stops being retyped in three places.

  • Custom tools and assistants

    When the tools you have can't do the job, we build something small and focused for a problem we've confirmed is worth solving.

  • Ongoing support

    Check-ins, fixes and tuning after launch, so what we build keeps working as your team and tools change.

How we work

One problem at a time

  1. 1

    Listen

    We talk through how the work really happens today, including what's slow, what gets dropped, and what tools you already use.

  2. 2

    Find the first win

    Together we pick one problem worth fixing and note where things stand now, so we can tell later whether it helped.

  3. 3

    Build or train

    We improve what you have, teach your team to use it well, or build something small if that's what it takes.

  4. 4

    Measure and support

    We check the result against where you started, and stay available to keep it running.
